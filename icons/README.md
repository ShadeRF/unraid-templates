# Icons

These are the icons and wordmarks of Filmhound and Recordhound. They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT license, and not by the apps' AGPL-3.0 license either.

You may use them to refer to the official apps: in an app list, a dashboard, a review or a link to the project. Please don't use them for other projects, for modified versions of the apps, or in a way that suggests you are the project.

The Community Apps templates and the apps' READMEs load these files by their address here, so they won't be renamed or removed.
